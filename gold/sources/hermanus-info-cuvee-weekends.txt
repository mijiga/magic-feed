Cuvée Weekends at Benguela Cove Wine Estate
Introducing Cuvée Weekends at Benguela Cove, a bottomless bubbly brunch that turns an ordinary weekend into something worth getting dressed up for.
Set against sweeping vineyard views, mountains and the Atlantic Ocean beyond, Moody Lagoon Restaurant at Benguela Cove Wine Estate invites guests to slow down, settle in and savour two beautifully indulgent hours of brunch and bubbles.
Crafted from grapes grown on the estate, this elegant Cap Classique brings a taste of Benguela Cove to every glass.
Cuvée Weekends at a glance
When: Every Saturday, Sunday and public holiday
Time: 11:00 to 13:00, with arrival slots at 11:00 and 11:30
Price: R295 per person
What's included: Two hours of freely poured Cuvée 58 Brut NV Cap Classique, served in 125 ml pours, with any brunch or lunch dish
Where: Moody Lagoon Restaurant, Benguela Cove Wine Estate, R43, Hermanus, approximately 90 minutes from Cape Town
Whether it's a long-overdue catch-up with friends, a romantic weekend date or a celebration that calls for something special, this is brunch with a little more sparkle.
Advance bookings are essential, and seating is limited. We encourage guests to reserve their preferred arrival time before tables fill up.
