That 80s Show Concert
That '80s Show, Live Music Performances at Benguela Cove Wine Estate
Mike McCullagh and The Harmonix will present a live, TV-style tribute concert celebrating the music and performers of the 1980s. Award-winning vocalists will recreate stars such as Freddie Mercury and Tina Turner, with music made famous by Queen, Journey, Madonna and other '80s favourites.
This is a dance-friendly concert rather than a formal seated show. Guests can dress in their best '80s outfits, sing along, visit the cash bar and dance until the encore.
