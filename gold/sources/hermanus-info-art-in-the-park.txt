HERMANUS ART IN THE PARK - FIRST SUNDAYS monthly
HERMANUS ART IN THE PARK
Art In The Park is open on the first Sunday of every month. 50 Artists will sell their work at the same venue as the Country Market. A perfect opportunity for buyers to invest in good quality art.
Art in the park has been set up as an opportunity for artists in Hermanus and the Overberg to exhibit their art.
A fabulous opportunity for everyone to view a huge variety of stunning original art which is available to purchase at affordable prices.
Farm Fresh breakfasts and delicious lunches will be available to enjoy in the beautiful Hermanus surroundings.
Event Courtesy of www.facebook.com/hermanusartinthepark/
